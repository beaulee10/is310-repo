# Web Scraping Assignment - Wookieepedia (Star Wars Wiki)

## Choice of Wiki
I chose the **Wookieepedia (Star Wars Fandom Wiki)** because it is one of the largest and most detailed fan-curated datasets in existence. I scraped the "Bounty Hunters" category.

## Research Interest
This data is valuable for digital humanists interested in character archetypes and the evolution of minor roles in expansive media franchises. Specifically, analyzing how characters are documented across "Canon" and "Legends" versions (as seen in my output) shows how fan communities manage competing timelines.

## Robots.txt
The robots.txt file for this wiki can be found here: https://starwars.fandom.com/robots.txt
I utilized `cloudscraper` to navigate bot protection while adhering to the allowed scraping paths for wiki articles.
